## What v43 and v46 change (from its declaration, §0.1 and §0.2)
Records lane, the part that reaches our scores:
- v43 removed the follow-up question (FQ) and its rewrite turn (we switched it off too).
- v46: the alias pass (AN, M91) reads only the packet records that name an asked name, and makes no call when none
  does: the same idea as our v11 alias scope.
- v46: no-record wordings (`EMAIL_NO_RECORD`, `UNNAMED_NO_RECORD`, `UNNAMED_REPLY`) apply only where the records do
  not state what was asked, no longer tell the model that this is so, and ask it to name whom the checked records
  are about rather than a list picked by code. This is our weakest datagen area (absence questions).
- v46: `MEMORY_LANE_SYSTEM` gains a passage: when the records give fewer of a number of things asked for, the reply
  gives those and the answer line holds just those.
- v46: the packet is a plain block per record instead of a JSON object, no pair id on the records lane (fewer tokens).
- v46: the answer slot is copied as written (v43 trimmed `*` and backticks).
- v46: search passes run under `SEARCH_SYSTEM` and the operator text, without the lane text.
Action and topic paths: reply-check and look-up wording changes, RR/RV removed, agreement and status forms as
separate system turns, L's reply passed on whenever it has text. Settings: packet 24,000, search passes 5,
identifier passes 6 (no cost cuts of ours or beast's).
