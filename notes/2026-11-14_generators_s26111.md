## How a list case is graded (applies to every list case below)
Checked offline with the official grader on hand-written replies (`tools/gradepack`, no model calls,
`/tmp/probe_memgen.py`, `/tmp/probe2.py`, `/tmp/probe3.py`). In order (`grade/v13.go:51-121`, `913-1005`):
1. **Dump guard** (`v13.go:101`): naming `DumpFloor(n)` distinct `dump_guard` values anywhere in answer+final_text scores 0.
   `DumpFloor` = max(4, ceil(n/2)) capped at n (`grade.go:722-734`). For memgen's 5-6 guards that is 4.
2. **Decline phrase anywhere** (`v13.go:104`): `list` is not an abstainable kind (`v13.go:164-170`). One phrase from
   `declinePhrases` (`grade.go:1161-1171`) or `declineV13EN` (`grade/v13_claims.go:162-171`), anywhere in the reply, makes
   the whole case **0**, even if both items are right. Measured:
   - Scores 0: "I can't find the second one", "I don't have a record of a second", "There is no record of the other
     one", "I'm not sure about the other one", "no mention of a second", "The second one isn't in your notes",
     "there's no third one", "I don't know the second".
   - Also 0 with both items correct: "A and B; there is no other cafe on file".
   - Not in the lexicon, so these keep 0.5: "I only found one", "I couldn't find the second one", "Only one cafe is
     recorded", "the records don't give a second", "no other cafe is mentioned".
3. **Distractor asserted** (`v13.go:962-974`): any `distractor_answers` value in an assertable clause (not rejected,
   past or echo) scores 0.
   - No answer slot: every sentence counts. "A and B. Gaky, a different person, goes to T." scores 0.
   - Answer slot set: only the slot, plus prose sentences that also hold a slot value, count
     (`v13_claims.go:614-687`). The same aside in its own sentence scores 1. "A and B, not T." also scores 1, because
     the "not" clause is rejected.
   - A distractor inside the slot always scores 0.
4. **Credit** = items found / items asked (`v13.go:997-1003`): 1 of 2 = 0.5, 1 of 3 = 0.33, 2 of 3 = 0.67.

For value cases (sn118gen's knowledge-update), asserting an older value also scores 0 (`v13.go:311-321`). A history
such as "you switched from X to Y and then to Z, so it's Z now" **scored 0**. "It was Y, but now it is Z" scored 1.

## 1. memgen v6 (5Hb5Sxe4, submission 01a9c68e; code says memgen version "8"; pinned c0 in bench 17)

### Families and weights
- One family only: `nick-list-2` at 35 of 35 (`gen/families.go:149`).
- Tier is always 1 (`gen/v3.go:415`, tiers {0,1,0}): the look-alike nicknames are bound to nobody.
- The defaults are the variant `s9`, "the v8 candidate" (`gen/variants.go:127-130`; knobs at `gen/v3.go:91-122`):
  - Looks = 3 look-alike nicknames per case.
  - Domains: cafes 2, bakeries 2, restaurants 2, bookshops 1, gyms 1, markets 1.
  - Identity notes: third-party and role, 1:1.
  - QForm "which"; PastRate 0.
  - RegisterTwins 5.
  - ChatterPerCase 17.
- Over the 3 packs: identity notes are third-party 50 and role 55. Every case's `run_after_wave` is 6, for user `ua`.

### Shape (pack-1, nick-list-2-t1-000 and -008)
- Question: "Which two cafes does Gwendolyn Castellano go to every week?"
- `answer_items`: [Blackthorn Tea Rooms, Alderbrook Roasters].
- Evidence for the third-party form (3 records, `v3.go:320-324`, `333`):
  - "Gwendolyn Castellano is at Alderbrook Roasters every week."
  - "Signe Larrabee always calls Gwendolyn Gak." The note gives the first name only.
  - "Gak is at Blackthorn Tea Rooms every week."
- Evidence for the role form (4 records, `v3.go:325-331`): "Esme Verhoeven is the new Zephyrwood darts team captain."
  then "The Zephyrwood darts team captain goes by Kip." then "Kip spends every week at Pinehaven Roasters."
- The direct item is phrased with varied verbs ("adores", "is at ... every week", "has a membership at ... and uses
  it"; `v3.go:46-84`). The records sit in random waves, so the nickname's item can come before the identity note.

### Traps and distractors
- **Look-alike nicknames**, one suffix away (`v3.go:162-192`, `families2.go:328-340`). Each carries its own
  same-frame item, with no binding note (`v3.go:337-354`): "Gaky is at Netherby Roasters every week.", "Gakie is at
  Cherrytree Coffee House every week.", "Gaks is at Wychwood Roasters every week."
  - Those items are the case's `distractor_answers`, so naming one scores 0.
  - The forms are shuffled before the real nickname is picked (`v3.go:271-273`). The real one can be the longer form,
    as with Lobs, Tedo and Kip. "Shortest form is the real one" is not a safe rule.
- **Twin distractors** (`v3.go:475-529`): up to 5 items from other cases that share a place word with a gold item.
  For gold "Gatehouse Leisure Centre", "Gatehouse Espresso Bar" is a distractor. That brings each case to 3-8
  distractors.
- **Dump guard** (`v3.go:426-455`): 5-6 same-domain items from other cases. Naming 4 of them scores 0.
- **Chatter** (`chatter.go:16-76`): 17 valueless notes per case about the full name, the nickname or a look-alike
  ("Gakie dropped off some spare jam jars for me."). That is about 600 of the about 835 pairs, so a name search
  mostly returns noise.

### Why an agent gets 0, 0.5 or 1 here
- **1:** the direct item plus the item filed under the bound nickname. The agent must resolve "X always calls
  <First> <Nick>" or role → nickname, match the exact nickname, never a one-suffix variant, and write no decline
  phrase.
- **0.5:** the direct item alone, stated plainly. This is DrPresident's uniform 0.5 = 17.5/35: it never resolves the
  nickname and never hedges or names a trap. "I only found one" or "Only one cafe is recorded" still scores 0.5.
- **0**, any one of these:
  - (a) A decline phrase anywhere, the usual hedge on a partial list ("A; I don't have a record of a second" or
    "there is no other"), even next to a correct answer.
  - (b) A look-alike's item or a twin item asserted. This includes taking Gaky, Gakie or Gaks for Gak, or putting
    such an item in the answer slot.
  - (c) 4 or more dump-guard items.
  - (d) Neither gold item.
- **Our agent (overhorizoner v3 / d42c-v16h): 0.20-0.26, with 0 on 17-22 of 35.** So the cases that do not score 0
  average about 0.5: we almost never resolve the nickname, and on half the cases something in the reply zeroes the
  half answer.
  - No per-case reply text exists for live columns, so the trigger is inferred, not observed.
  - `d42c/app/records.py:36` routes "none of the records states what was asked" to `NO_RECORD_REPLY` ("say plainly that
    you have no record of it", `memory/deliberation.py:5`). Applied to the missing second item, that is a decline
    phrase, so 0.
  - The same prompt says a value belonging to someone else may be mentioned "and that it is not what was asked". With
    the ANSWER slot set and the aside in its own sentence that is safe (scored 1 locally). Without the slot it is 0.
  - Confirming which trigger fires needs a run on `s26111-memgen-v6` (paid; not done).

### What an agent needs to score high
1. Answer a counted list with the items found, and nothing about the ones not found. No "no record", "not sure",
   "can't find", "there is no", "isn't in your notes" or "don't have" anywhere, including after a full answer.
2. Resolve the identity note in both forms:
   - First name only: "<Other> always calls <First> <Nick>". First names are unique in the pack (`builder.go:127-141`).
   - Role chain: "<Full> is the new <role>." then "The <role> goes by <Nick>."
   - Then take the item stated under that exact nickname.
3. Treat names as exact tokens. Gak, Gaky, Gakie and Gaks are four different people. Never infer a binding from
   spelling.
4. Put only the gold items in the ANSWER slot. Keep any mention of other people's items out of the sentence that
   holds the answer, or leave them out entirely.
5. Never list the domain's other items, such as all the cafes in memory.

### Traps
Three distractors per case, and no dump guard:
- Two look-alike nicknames, each **explicitly bound to someone else** ("Everyone at the climbing wall knows Ilan
  Zubiri as Cubbyers."), each with an item: "Cubbyers is a total regular at Rookery Row Vinyl Exchange." The suffixes

- A same-surname relative's item, in a sentence that names the target: "Quentin Vasquez's sibling, Ragnhild Vasquez, is
  a total regular at Dorrin Common Vinyl Exchange."

### Grading (bench 18)
- All three items score 1.
- Two items score 0.67, the only partial that counts as solved. One item scores 0.33.
- Any distractor, or any decline phrase, scores 0. "there's no third one" scored 0. "I couldn't find a third" kept
  0.67.
- The relative's item in a separate sentence scores 0 without a slot and 1 with one.

### Agent needs
- Resolve both nicknames, including initials such as "X.S." or "W.J.", which a capitalised-word regex misses.
- Never take the relative's item from the target-naming sentence.
- Give a partial list with no hedge.

## 4. sn118gen v4 (5HQsecKg, submission 206867a5; code version 7; bench 17 column)

### Mix
- 15 of 35 cases (`BenchShare = 30/70`, `main.go:32`, `46`) come from the **official DittoBench generator**.
  - It runs at its own salted seed, on the v15 full-profile dataset, because `min(grading, 15)` is used
    (`gen/bench.go:44-59`).
  - Cases are taken round-robin under `typeCaps` (`bench.go:273-289`). Five types are skipped (`bench.go:294-306`):
    reported-speech, retraction, story-order, declarative-behavior and status-current.
  - These cases keep the dataset's typo'd text ("How lnog is the upddatsd stay in Italy..."). They are seeded for
    users `miner` and `colleague`, and run after wave 0.
- The other 20 cases come from the **V7 own families** (`families2.go:85`, `families4.go:183`), for user `u1` in 4 folded
  waves with 14 filler pairs per wave:
  - 4 each of MarriedName, HandleBridge and ParentBridge. These are 2-item lists, type `multi-hop`.
  - 2 FirstNameEmail (`multi-hop` value).
  - 2 each of Renegotiated, HandleBack and LiveSwitch (`knowledge-update` values).
- Bench types seen in pack-1:
  - 2 each: absence-twin-false-premise, absence-twin-stale-removed, v13-open-program, v13-personal-program,
    world-story-x-owner-email.
  - 1 each: point-in-time-contact, point-in-time-trip-leg, world-story-quantity, world-story-owner-current,
    parser-divergence-negation.

### Own families: question, evidence and trap (pack-1)
- **MarriedName** (`families4.go:115-131`):
  - Question: "Which two card games does Marian Beaumont love, as far as you know?"
  - Evidence: "Marian Beaumont loves Baccarat, easily their favourite card game." Then "Marian Beaumont married Yuna
    Montague ... goes by Marian Montague now." Then "Marian Montague has been really into Sheepshead lately".
  - Traps: "Marion Montague" (the spouse's sister, one letter off) with Pinochle, and "Mariana Beaumont" with Tarocchi.
- **HandleBridge** (`136-154`): "Sefu Oyelaran's personal email is s.oyelaran29@fastmail.com." Then "the account
  registered to s.oyelaran29@... plays as velvetanchor." Then "velvetanchor has been really into Otello". The traps are
  `velvetanchor_` and `velvetanchor2`.
- **ParentBridge** (`159-180`): "Alistair Galloway's daughter Nuria ..." then "Nuria's dad has been really into
  Caslon". The traps are "Nurit's dad" (Avenir) and "Nuria's grandad" (Frutiger).
- Grading for these three: as in "How a list case is graded". Both items score 1, the direct item scores 0.5, and any
  trap or decline phrase scores 0. "As far as I know, only Baccarat" scored 0.5; "...; I don't know the second" scored 0.
- **Renegotiated** (`families3.go:18-33`): three supplier changes on one booking code. The last change reads "went back

- **LiveSwitch** (`88-106`): "I switched my vet to ..." x3-4, asked as "currently". Only the newest is gold. A
  recited history scores 0.
- **FirstNameEmail** (`64-83`): two Mariselas at two employers. The emails are stated under first name plus employer,
  and the question uses the full name. The other Marisela's email is the distractor.

### Agent needs
- Follow one bridge hop: married name, email → game tag, or "<kid>'s dad".
- Treat near-identical names (Marion/Marian, `tag_`/`tag2`, Nurit/Nuria) as different people. Ignore the grandad.
- Give the newest value only: no recited history and no "went back to" = revert rule.
- Answer the full name through the nickname, and first name plus employer.
- Plus the usual official-datagen skills for the 15 bench cases: typo-robust reading, case-file programs, point in time,
  story owner/email, and absence twins.
