## Metamorphic consistency

The share of twin groups answered consistently (all members correct or all wrong); a split group is
the penalty. Max penalty 0.4 on v14 (0.9385 factor at 0.846), so with 13 groups each split group costs
about 3.1% of the composite, while one more question right is worth about 0.2%.

The 13 counted groups are exactly the program families: v13-personal-program (6 groups),
v13-open-program (4), enterprise-composed-program (3), three rewordings each of one case-file question.
Every program twin group is its own case file, and all its members expect the same answer (checked on
six seeds); counterfactual cases sit in other case files.

M2 (filing-date order) raised program accuracy but split groups: seed 3555, v5g 0 split of 13 (wrong
groups were wrong on all rewordings) vs v7a 4 split of 10 counted. The splits come from steps that run
per rewording after the shared reading and draft: the final check flipped a right answer on one
rewording (c7ab8a4833c150dbb) and misread a case-file id on another (ce8a8ddf2790257f4).

The served questions of one group differ only in the preface and the format sentence; asked_part (the
key of the shared draft) is identical across them.

## Conversational sanity

Geometric mean of the `correct` rates of conversational-chitchat, conversational-declarative and
declarative-behavior (three questions each), folded as 0.25 + 0.75 x metric (the v7 contract's floor;
scorer.go's convSanityFloor 0.5 is the older one): one miss in a slice gives 0.874 and a factor of
0.905, locally and on the platform. Chitchat scores 0.5 but counts as correct. M1b targets the declarative misses.

## Inference cost (shadow, not applied)

On the platform every case is attributed: 214 of 350 below the full factor, mean factor 0.803, floor
0.6; budgets are 3 completions for a memory case, 3 for a single tool case, 5 for a chain. If the
operators enforce it, horizon v1 loses about 20%. Nothing in /public/bench/rollout or /config says when.

## Composed program: why it is left as it is

Every composed trajectory of today's v14 runs (v5g full run on 3555, the eight v7a gate runs; 84 in
all, practice/gates/composed.py): 80 right. Long-packet mode (effort low) is where 80% of them run and
is no worse (64/67 against 16/17). The four misses have four different causes: a miscount (2 counted
as 1), a final turn that answered a distractor contact although all four search notes gave the right
one, a final reply that listed the records instead of one value, and a first search pass that timed out
after 96 s and starved passes 2-5 (4 of 190 records-lane questions had a failed pass; this was the only
one that declined).

The fixes that would catch them act on the answer: overriding a final answer that disagrees with the
search notes, or asking again when a reply lacks its ANSWER line. That is the content-based override /
retry that screening rule I3 rejects (horizon v2 was rejected for a milder note), so they are out.
Sharing an answer across the composed rewordings is out too: each is its own case file, and a
causal-counterfactual case asks the same question of other facts. A deterministic solver for the
entity records would be a benchmark parser. Expected cost left: about 0.4 split groups per run (-1.2%).
