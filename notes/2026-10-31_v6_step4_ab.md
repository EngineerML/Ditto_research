**F1 works.** v30's open-program split on seed 4137 came back in its rerun, so it split that group in both of its samples. In v6 the final check never fired on an answered program reply, and every open-program question was right.

**F3 half works.** The task is now always on the answer line ("Katherine Phelps ; share the final headcount ; chqt"). The notes' typing slips are still copied ("chqt", "ejail", "canfe order"), and a channel sometimes still carries "before the end of the month". The spelling sentence is not enough for this model.

**F2 failed.** Results on the revert group (Makenzie, Ariana, Vincent, then "reverts to the previous entry instead; the newest entry governs"):
- v30: 0 right of 6;
- first wording: 1 of 6, which turned an all-wrong group into a split;
- reworded: 0 of 9.

The model reads "the previous entry" as the entry just before the revert note. A part-right twin group costs about 3% of the composite, against the 3 questions an all-wrong group costs, so a fix that works only sometimes is worse than none. Both wordings are reverted, and `CHAIN_NOTE` is v30's again.
