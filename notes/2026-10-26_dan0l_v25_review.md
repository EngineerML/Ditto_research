## Rules

A request-keyed table of instructions per scored family is what the older I5 text names
("phrase tables", "family-specific instruction compilers"). The 2026-10-23 "routed instruction
guidance" clarification allows routing advisory guidance by phrasing when the model still decides,
tools are not forced, and no benchmark-private knowledge is encoded; v25 was admitted under it.
The notes encode grader expectations for specific families and some triggers quote competition
text, so a stricter rescreen (rescreen_scored covers every scored row) or policy v14 could read it
as benchmark emulation. Copying it would also tie our code to dan0l's newest lineage while v26 is
still under embargo, and it goes against this project's rule of no benchmark-derived routing banks.
