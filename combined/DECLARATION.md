# Pipeline declaration

Every model call and every host step on the served path, with the lines that show them.
No model weights or opaque components; the only model is hosted and called over HTTP.

## 2. Model calls

All go through `OpenAICompatLLM.chat` (`app/llm.py:242`) on the request's relay URL (`app/agent.py:83-84`), else the configured endpoint
(`app/agent.py:85-87`, `app/llm.py:343-361`). Payload: model (`app/config.py:66`), messages, tools with `tool_choice` `auto` when
any, `temperature` 0 and `seed` 42 (`app/config.py:42-43`), reasoning effort and `max_tokens` (`app/llm.py:243-256`); only D's drafts set
`temperature` 0.7, `seed` 43/44 (configured `seed` + index − 1) and `tool_choice` `none` (`app/llm.py:243`, `app/llm.py:254`,
`app/memory/deliberation.py:71-72`); DR's drafts (M50) set `temperature` 0.8 and `seed` 101/202 and keep A1's `tool_choice` (`app/action.py:84-85`, `app/action.py:515`). No `n`, `best_of` or field outside the relay's forwarded and pinned lists. A call the model made is repeated to its later turns as the model wrote it, except that an argument with an empty name is left out of that copy, since the provider refuses a conversation that repeats one; the endpoint and `tool_calls` get the call unchanged (`app/action.py:345-349`). A draft is sent once and never again (`app/llm.py:312`, `app/llm.py:329-330`); any other call is sent again only on a transport outcome:
failed or timed-out connection (`app/llm.py:277-290`), 408/409/425/429/5xx (`app/llm.py:320-328`), a 200 that is not a completion
(`app/llm.py:299-303`); 429 or 503 with `Retry-After` is waited out, ≤5 s × 30 (`app/llm.py:312-319`); after a timeout, 0.4 of `max_tokens`,
never under 1200 (`app/llm.py:287-288`). A completion is returned as it came (`app/llm.py:305-309`); text parts are joined, a nameless call
skipped (`app/llm.py:126-134`). All usage, failed calls' included, enters the case tally before the reply is read (R1 `app/records.py:185-188`, S `app/records.py:292-295`, N `app/records.py:333-337`, T `app/records.py:354-361`, R3 `app/records.py:466-470`, D `app/memory/deliberation.py:87-99`, A `app/action.py:579-583`, RA `app/action.py:136-139`, RC `app/action.py:174-177`, O, SD and H `app/short.py:134-140`; P and Q at R3's site).

## 4. Fixed properties

- `final_text` and `answer` are the model's text with outer whitespace removed, the two hyphen characters of M22 written as the
  plain hyphen and the bold markers M24 removes removed; nothing else is written, cut, joined or re-worded (§1).
- No reply is chosen among others by what it says, and no stage is asked again because of what its reply says (M5, M17, M35: RC is armed by a reply's shape, its verdict is a separate model's, and the deciding model may keep its reply; M32: FQ is armed by DC's reply's shape, and the reply after its note gives way to DC's only when it is empty; M36: the final check is armed by the path and the reply's shape, and the reply after its note gives way to the one before only when it is empty); RA gets
  one reminder when its first word is neither `SEND` nor `HOLD`. The shared reply SR (M52) is chosen by its key alone: the host neither reads nor compares the replies it passes on.
- Every turn of R1, R3/R4, DC, P, Q and A1/A2, the closing turns included, carries the whole catalog (M3). The calls without tools
  are RA (M2), RC (M35), RR (M30), AC (M37), RV (M38), L (M33), FQ (M32) and the passes O, SD, T, N and H, whose text is never the reply; DR's drafts (M50) are offered A1's list, their calls are never posted and their text is never the reply.
- A held call is not posted by the host on its own, and an earlier result is not returned as a new one (M1).
- An unsent call (unparsable, outside the catalog, failed schema, held by RA or after AC, out of time) gets a note as its result in R3/R4, P, Q
  and A1/A2 and is logged `blocked_before_execution` (M12; `app/action.py:630-643`, `app/records.py:540-567`,
  `app/action.py:650-652`, `app/records.py:574-576`), as are a draft's calls; R1 drops only unsendable calls, logs an RA hold (`app/records.py:226`);
  a nameless call is logged, not run (`app/llm.py:133`). None of these checks reads the request's words or case id.
- Of the request the host reads the case id, the operator's `system_prompt`, the user's message, the catalog, the tool endpoint, the user id and the
  relay URL (`app/protocol.py:25-32`); the case id goes only into the body sent to the tool endpoint (`app/tools.py:149`) and into log lines.
- A call that was not sent is not reported as sent, and a refused call is not reported as executed (M14).

- DK (M42) is also a call without tools, and its text is never the reply; a call it holds gets `DUPLICATE_CHECK_NOTE` as its result in A1 and is logged `blocked_before_execution` (`app/action.py:645-647`).
